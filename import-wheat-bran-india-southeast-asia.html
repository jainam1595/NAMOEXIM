Sourcing Premium Wheat Bran from India: A Guide for Southeast Asian Buyers

Southeast Asia's rapidly growing livestock sector demands reliable, high-volume feed ingredients. Wheat Bran, owing to its high phosphorus content and excellent laxative properties, is indispensable for dairy and pregnant cattle diets in tropical climates.

Logistical Advantages of Indian Wheat Bran

For buyers in Vietnam, Indonesia, and Malaysia, importing from India offers significantly shorter transit times compared to sourcing from the Americas or Eastern Europe. This shorter sea freight reduces the risk of moisture accumulation and mold growth during transit.

Quality Assurance at NAMO EXIM

We ensure our Wheat Bran is produced from premium Indian wheat, maintaining a coarse texture that is highly preferred by cattle. Our quality control guarantees zero adulteration with sand, silica, or urea.
