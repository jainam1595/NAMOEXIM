How Cottonseed Oil Cake Dramatically Increases Milk Yield in Dairy Cows

For commercial dairy farms, increasing milk yield and butterfat percentage is the ultimate goal. Balancing a dairy cow's diet to meet high energy demands during peak lactation can be challenging, but one of the most effective cattle feed ingredients to achieve this is Cottonseed Oil Cake.

Derived from the extraction of oil from cotton seeds, this dense, nutrient-rich cake has become a staple for dairy farmers looking to maximize output and maintain herd health.

The Power of Bypass Protein

Not all proteins are created equal when it comes to ruminant digestion. Cottonseed Cake is unique because it provides a high level of "bypass protein cattle" can utilize efficiently. This refers to protein that escapes microbial digestion in the rumen and is absorbed directly in the lower intestine (abomasum).

Because the amino acids are absorbed directly into the bloodstream, they directly support tissue repair and, most importantly, stimulate higher milk production without causing digestive upset or acidosis in the rumen.

Natural Fat for Energy & Milk Butterfat

Dairy cows exert massive amounts of energy. The residual oil in cottonseed oil cake for cows acts as a highly dense energy source. This helps cows maintain optimal body condition scores (BCS) during peak lactation periods when they are often in a negative energy balance.

Furthermore, the specific fatty acid profile found in cottonseed oil naturally boosts the overall butterfat percentage of the milk, leading to higher profitability for farmers selling milk based on fat content.
