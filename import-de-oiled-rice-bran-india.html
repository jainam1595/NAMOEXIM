Why Indian De-Oiled Rice Bran (DORB) is the Top Choice for Cattle Feed Importers

As global demand for cost-effective animal feed ingredients rises, De-Oiled Rice Bran (DORB) has become a staple for cattle feed manufacturers worldwide. India, being one of the largest rice producers globally, is the premier hub for sourcing high-quality DORB.

Unmatched Nutritional Profile

Indian DORB offers a consistent crude protein level of 14-16%, making it a superb base ingredient. More importantly, stringent milling processes in India ensure low silica content (maximum 2.5%), which prevents digestive issues in ruminants.
