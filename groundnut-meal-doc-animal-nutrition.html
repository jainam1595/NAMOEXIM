Groundnut Meal (DOC) in Animal Nutrition: High Protein for Maximum Growth

When rapid weight gain and muscle development are the goals, few plant-based ingredients can match the raw nutritional power of Groundnut Meal (De-oiled Cake).

A 50% Protein Powerhouse

Groundnut Meal is exceptionally protein-dense, often reaching 45% to 50% crude protein. It is highly palatable and exudes a sweet, nutty aroma that stimulates feed intake in calves, piglets, and poultry.

Safe Sourcing Practices

The primary concern with peanut byproducts globally is aflatoxin contamination. At NAMO EXIM, we source only from state-of-the-art facilities with strict moisture controls and rigorous lab testing to ensure 100% safe, export-grade Groundnut DOC.
