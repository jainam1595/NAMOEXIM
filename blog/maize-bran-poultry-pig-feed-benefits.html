Maize Bran for Pig and Poultry Feed: Nutritional Benefits & Import Guide

When formulating diets for monogastric animals like pigs and poultry, finding cost-effective, bulk energy sources is critical for profitability. Maize Bran (also known as corn bran) is an outstanding fibrous energy source that significantly lowers formulation costs while providing essential dietary fiber.

As a by-product of the maize milling industry, it consists primarily of the outer coating of the corn kernel. Let's explore why this is becoming one of the most sought-after pig feed ingredients and poultry diet staples globally.

Digestibility and Gut Health

Maize bran animal feed is highly palatable and contains highly digestible fiber. In swine diets, this fiber acts as a prebiotic. It ferments in the hindgut, promoting healthy gut flora, improving overall digestion, and helping prevent gastric ulcers in growing pigs.

For poultry, particularly in layer diets, it serves as an excellent poultry feed energy source that provides necessary physical bulk to the feed without diluting essential nutrients, promoting better gut motility.

Nutritional Profile Breakdown

  • Crude Protein: 8% - 10%
  • Crude Fiber: 10% - 12% (Highly digestible)
  • Fat/Oil Content: 3% - 5%
  • Energy: High metabolizable energy ideal for monogastrics.

Sourcing and Importing from India

For feed manufacturers looking to import maize bran India is a premier destination. Indian Maize Bran is favored globally due to the country's massive non-GMO corn production and competitive export pricing (often traded under HS code 23021010).
