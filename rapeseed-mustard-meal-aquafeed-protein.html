Rapeseed Meal: A Cost-Effective Plant Protein Source for Commercial Aquafeed

As the global aquaculture industry seeks sustainable alternatives to expensive marine fishmeal, Rapeseed (Mustard) Meal has emerged as a powerhouse plant protein.

Excellent Amino Acid Balance

Indian Rapeseed Meal boasts a crude protein content of 36-38% with a highly favorable amino acid profile, particularly rich in methionine—an essential amino acid often lacking in other plant proteins like soybean meal.

Application in Fish & Shrimp Feed

When properly formulated, Rapeseed Meal can replace a significant portion of fishmeal in diets for Carp, Tilapia, and Shrimp without affecting growth performance, offering massive cost savings to feed mills.
