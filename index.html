About NAMO EXIM

India's Trusted Agricultural Feed Exporter

Navkar Traders has been continuously serving the domestic cattle feed industry for over 27 years. Built on trust, quality, and reliability, we have established long-lasting relationships with farmers, livestock owners, and businesses.

Our deep market experience and commitment to customer satisfaction have made us a trusted name in the industry. NAMO EXIM is an India-based exporter of premium agricultural feed ingredients and cattle feed products. We specialize in supplying De-oiled Rice Bran, Groundnut Meal, Rapeseed Meal, Cottonseed Oil Cake, Maize Bran, and Wheat Bran to international markets.

Our mission is to build long-term business partnerships through trust, consistency, and quality-focused export services. We serve clients across Sri Lanka, Bangladesh, Singapore, Malaysia, Indonesia, Thailand, and Vietnam.
